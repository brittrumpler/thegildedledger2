Privacy & your journal

Last updated: November 10, 2026.

Local progress

Bookmarks, haul quantities, time zone, imported game progress, manual completion notes and wish lists are stored in this browser’s local storage. You can download a journal backup or delete your local journal from My Pirate.

Optional Microsoft / Xbox sign-in

When the site owner enables sign-in, Microsoft handles authentication. The service exchanges authorization codes and Xbox tokens on its server to identify your Xbox user ID and gamertag. Login credentials are entered on Microsoft’s website. Service tokens are discarded after the identity lookup. An encrypted, Secure, HttpOnly session cookie expires after twelve hours; a separate short-lived cookie protects the sign-in flow.

Official profile import

The optional export bookmark runs only on your signed-in official Sea of Thieves profile. It requests overview, reputation, Chest, balance and achievements, removes credential fields, and downloads a JSON file to your computer. It never reads browser cookies or sends your export to another website. When you import the file here, the app keeps only the normalized progress, owned-item matches, limited identity metadata and matching report needed for your journal.

Optional cloud journal

If the owner enables Cloudflare KV storage, you can explicitly save your journal to the connected Xbox account. Private journals are stored under the authenticated Xbox user ID. You can load or delete the saved cloud copy from My Pirate. A cloud save is optional; local tracking works without it. These imports are user-provided snapshots, not verified attestations of current game state.

Public guides and images

Cosmetic images and detailed unlock guides are loaded on demand from the catalog’s public source. On Cloudflare, the companion can cache and proxy approved image sources. A static-only deployment may load those images directly from SoTDB or the Sea of Thieves Wiki. Their servers receive ordinary image requests. The original treasure image packs are bundled locally.

Data coverage

Catalog coverage and review dates are shown in the journal. Missing data stays unknown. Unlocking a cosmetic for purchase is tracked separately from owning it. Your Microsoft sign-in alone does not automatically read your official Sea of Thieves progress or your live game session.

Site operation

This release includes no advertising or analytics scripts. Cloudflare and Microsoft process service and security data under their own policies. If you operate or modify this website, keep this notice accurate for your deployment and provide an appropriate contact method to your users.
